kirinuki

kirinuki is a personal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel holo-jp-clips to upload videos that the owner has edited (English-subtitled clips) to that channel.

It runs only on the owner's own computer and is not offered to other users. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and thumbnail.
